Call for Papers

AI for practical industrial transformation

AI4I invites original submissions on the design, deployment, evaluation, and governance of AI solutions in industrial settings. The workshop welcomes research papers, short papers, position papers, experience reports, industrial case studies, tool demonstrations, dataset papers, benchmark papers, and lessons learned from real-world deployments.

Scope

Artificial intelligence is increasingly used to support automation, optimization, quality control, maintenance, planning, monitoring, and decision-making across industrial sectors. However, applying AI in industry requires more than accurate models. Solutions must be reliable, scalable, explainable, safe, secure, cost-effective, and possible to integrate with existing workflows and legacy systems.

AI4I welcomes contributions that address these challenges in domains such as manufacturing, energy, mobility, healthcare, logistics, construction, agriculture, telecommunications, smart cities, and other industrial contexts. Submissions should clearly describe the industrial problem, the proposed AI approach, the evaluation setting, and the contribution to both research and practice.

Accepted papers will be published in the NGEN-AI 2026 main conference proceedings in Springer CCIS series, indexed in leading databases including SCOPUS, Norwegian Register for Scientific Journals and Series, DBLP, EI Compendex, INSPEC, SCImago, zbMATH, and the Japanese Science and Technology Agency (JST).
Topics of Interest

Relevant topics include, but are not limited to

  • AI for manufacturing, production, and process optimization
  • Predictive maintenance and anomaly detection in industrial systems
  • Industrial IoT, edge AI, and cloud-edge intelligence
  • Digital twins, simulation, and AI-enabled monitoring
  • Computer vision for inspection, quality control, and safety
  • Generative AI and LLMs for industrial workflows
  • Agentic AI, autonomous operations, and decision support
  • Trustworthy, explainable, safe, and responsible AI for industry
  • Human-AI collaboration in industrial environments
  • AI for logistics, supply chains, and resource planning
  • Federated learning and privacy-preserving AI across industrial sites
  • MLOps, lifecycle management, monitoring, and maintenance of industrial AI
  • AI for energy efficiency, sustainability, and circular industry
  • Cybersecurity, resilience, and risk management for industrial AI
  • Industrial datasets, benchmarks, testbeds, and reproducibility
