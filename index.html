AI for Industry

The International Workshop on AI for Industry (AI4I 2026) is organized with the 2026 International Conference on Next Generation AI Systems (NGEN-AI 2026), taking place on 1–4 September 2026 in Trento, Italy, and online over Zoom.

AI4I is a forum for researchers, practitioners, technology providers, and industrial stakeholders working on the design, deployment, evaluation, and governance of AI solutions in real industrial environments.

Call for Papers

Bridging AI innovation and industrial transformation.

AI4I invites original submissions that explore how artificial intelligence can create value in industrial contexts while addressing practical challenges such as reliability, scalability, safety, trustworthiness, integration, cost, data quality, and organizational adoption.

Scope

AI is reshaping industrial sectors by enabling intelligent automation, predictive maintenance, quality inspection, resource optimization, supply-chain intelligence, autonomous operations, and advanced decision support. However, moving AI from prototypes to production requires more than model accuracy. Industrial AI solutions must operate under real constraints, including legacy systems, limited data, safety requirements, domain-specific regulations, explainability needs, cybersecurity risks, and changing operational environments.
